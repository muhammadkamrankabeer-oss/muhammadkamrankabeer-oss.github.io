Chapter 1Introduction to Computational SystemsOpen notes

Systems, computer components, number representation and software

Practise Class 9 Computer Science MCQs →Back to chapter contents ↑

Short Notes

A computational system combines hardware, software, data and people to receive input, process it and produce output. Hardware is the physical part; software gives instructions. Computers represent information using binary digits (bits).

Important concepts

  • A system has components, relationships and a purpose.
  • CPU processes instructions; memory and storage keep data.
  • Binary uses 0 and 1; one byte usually contains 8 bits.

Chapter Summary in Easy Words

A computational system combines hardware, software, data and people to receive input, process it and produce output. Hardware is the physical part; software gives instructions. Computers represent information using binary digits (bits). In revision, focus on the key terms, understand why each concept is used, and practise explaining it with your own example.

Important Definitions and Key Terms

TermEasy definition
SystemA group of connected parts working together for a purpose.
HardwareThe physical components of a computer.
SoftwarePrograms and instructions that tell a computer what to do.
BitThe smallest unit of digital data; it is 0 or 1.

Important Short Questions with Answers

What is a computational system?

A system that uses computing components to accept input, process data, store it and produce output.

Differentiate hardware and software.

Hardware can be touched, such as a keyboard. Software is a set of instructions, such as an operating system.

What is binary number system?

A base-2 number system that uses only the digits 0 and 1.

Why are input and output important?

Input supplies data or commands; output presents the result of processing.

Chapter 2System Design and TroubleshootingOpen notes

Logic design, problem diagnosis and systematic repair

Practise Class 9 Computer Science MCQs →Back to chapter contents ↑

Short Notes

System design means planning how components work together to meet a need. Logic gates perform basic Boolean operations. Troubleshooting is a step-by-step process: identify the problem, form a likely cause, test it, apply a fix and verify the system.

Important concepts

  • Change one thing at a time while troubleshooting.
  • AND: all inputs must be 1; OR: at least one input is 1.
  • Verify that the system works after repair.

Chapter Summary in Easy Words

System design means planning how components work together to meet a need. Logic gates perform basic Boolean operations. Troubleshooting is a step-by-step process: identify the problem, form a likely cause, test it, apply a fix and verify the system. In revision, focus on the key terms, understand why each concept is used, and practise explaining it with your own example.

Important Definitions and Key Terms

TermEasy definition
Logic gateAn electronic circuit that performs a Boolean operation.
Truth tableA table showing outputs for every possible input combination.
TroubleshootingA systematic process for finding and fixing a problem.
AND gateA logic gate that outputs 1 only when all its inputs are 1.

Important Short Questions with Answers

What is troubleshooting?

A structured method of identifying a fault, testing possible causes, applying a solution and checking the result.

What does an OR gate do?

It outputs 1 when at least one input is 1.

Why test a proposed solution?

Testing checks whether the suspected cause was correct and whether the fix works.

What is a truth table used for?

It lists all input combinations and the output of a logic circuit.

Chapter 3Introduction to Computer NetworksOpen notes

Network purposes, devices, media, addressing and protocols

Practise Class 9 Computer Science MCQs →Back to chapter contents ↑

Short Notes

A computer network connects devices so they can communicate and share resources. LAN covers a small area; WAN covers a large geographical area. Switches connect devices in a LAN, routers forward traffic between networks, and protocols define communication rules.

Important concepts

  • A switch connects devices within a LAN; a router connects networks.
  • Wired and wireless media both carry data.
  • Use strong passwords and updates to improve network security.

Chapter Summary in Easy Words

A computer network connects devices so they can communicate and share resources. LAN covers a small area; WAN covers a large geographical area. Switches connect devices in a LAN, routers forward traffic between networks, and protocols define communication rules. Important Definitions and Key Terms

TermEasy definition
NetworkConnected devices that exchange data and share resources.
LANA local area network covering a limited area.
RouterA device that forwards packets between IP networks.
ProtocolA set of rules for communication between devices.

Important Short Questions with Answers

Give two benefits of a network.

Networks support resource sharing and communication, such as sharing printers and files.

Differentiate LAN and WAN.

A LAN covers a limited place; a WAN connects networks across larger distances.

What is the role of a router?

It forwards data packets between different networks.

Why are protocols needed?

They define how devices format, send, receive and interpret data.

Chapter 4Computational ThinkingOpen notes

Decomposition, pattern recognition, abstraction and algorithms

Practise Class 9 Computer Science MCQs →Back to chapter contents ↑

Short Notes

Computational thinking helps break a complex problem into manageable steps. Decomposition divides a problem, pattern recognition finds similarities, abstraction focuses on important details, and algorithm design creates a clear sequence of steps.

Important concepts

  • Use a start/end oval, process rectangle, decision diamond and flow arrows.
  • Pseudocode describes steps without strict programming-language syntax.
  • Test algorithms with normal, boundary and unusual inputs.

Chapter Summary in Easy Words

Computational thinking helps break a complex problem into manageable steps. Decomposition divides a problem, pattern recognition finds similarities, abstraction focuses on important details, and algorithm design creates a clear sequence of steps. In revision, focus on the key terms, understand why each concept is used, and practise explaining it with your own example.

Important Definitions and Key Terms

TermEasy definition
DecompositionBreaking a large problem into smaller parts.
Pattern recognitionFinding similarities or repeated features.
AbstractionFocusing on relevant details and ignoring unnecessary ones.
AlgorithmA finite, ordered set of steps to solve a problem.

Important Short Questions with Answers

Name four computational-thinking concepts.

Decomposition, pattern recognition, abstraction and algorithm design.

What makes an algorithm useful?

It should be clear, ordered, finite and produce the intended result.

What is a flowchart?

A diagram that represents an algorithm using standard symbols.

Differentiate algorithm and program.

An algorithm is a solution plan; a program is that plan written in a programming language.

Collecting, cleaning, analysing and presenting data

Practise Class 9 Computer Science MCQs →Back to chapter contents ↑

Short Notes

Data science uses data to answer questions and support decisions. Data gathering collects relevant observations; cleaning addresses missing, duplicated or incorrect values. Analysis finds patterns, while tables and charts communicate results clearly.

Important concepts

  • Start with a clear question and collect relevant data.
  • Choose charts according to the type of comparison.
  • Label chart titles, axes and units.

Chapter Summary in Easy Words

Data science uses data to answer questions and support decisions. Data gathering collects relevant observations; cleaning addresses missing, duplicated or incorrect values. Analysis finds patterns, while tables and charts communicate results clearly. In revision, focus on the key terms, understand why each concept is used, and practise explaining it with your own example.

Important Definitions and Key Terms

TermEasy definition
DataRaw facts, measurements or observations.
Data cleaningFinding and correcting errors, duplicates or missing values.
MeanThe sum of values divided by the number of values.
VisualizationPresenting information using charts or graphs.

Important Short Questions with Answers

Differentiate data and information.

Data is raw facts; information is data processed into a useful form.

Why clean data before analysis?

Errors and duplicates can distort results and lead to incorrect conclusions.

When is a bar chart useful?

It compares values across separate categories.

Find the mean of 4, 6 and 8.

(4 + 6 + 8) ÷ 3 = 6.

Chapter 7Emerging Technologies in Computer ScienceOpen notes

AI, IoT, cloud computing, robotics and big data

Practise Class 9 Computer Science MCQs →Back to chapter contents ↑

Short Notes

Emerging technologies are developing tools that change how people work and communicate. Artificial intelligence performs tasks associated with human intelligence; IoT connects physical devices; cloud computing provides computing resources over networks. These technologies offer benefits but also raise privacy, security and fairness concerns.

Important concepts

  • Learn the purpose, uses and limitations of each technology.
  • AI outputs can be wrong or biased; verify important results.
  • Protect personal data and use secure settings.

Important Definitions and Key Terms

TermEasy definition
Artificial intelligence (AI)Technology that enables machines to perform tasks requiring abilities such as learning or reasoning.
Internet of Things (IoT)Physical devices connected to networks to collect or exchange data.
Cloud computingUsing computing resources such as storage or servers over a network.
Big dataVery large or complex datasets that need specialized methods to analyse.

Important Short Questions with Answers

What is AI?

AI refers to systems that perform tasks such as recognizing patterns, understanding language or making predictions.

Give one IoT example.

A connected temperature sensor that sends readings to a monitoring application.

State one benefit and one risk of cloud computing.

It offers flexible access to resources; risks can include privacy concerns or dependence on connectivity.

Why should emerging technologies be used responsibly?

They may affect privacy, security, employment, fairness and the environment.

Chapter 8Ethical, Social, and Legal Concerns in Computer UsageOpen notes

Privacy, security, intellectual property and responsible use

Practise Class 9 Computer Science MCQs →Back to chapter contents ↑

Short Notes

Responsible computer use respects people, data, laws and ownership. Users should protect private information, use strong authentication, avoid plagiarism, respect copyright and behave respectfully online. Cyberbullying, piracy and unauthorized access can cause harm and may break rules or laws.

Important concepts

  • Ask permission before sharing another person’s information or images.
  • Credit sources and respect software licences.
  • Report harmful content and keep evidence when appropriate.

Chapter Summary in Easy Words

Responsible computer use respects people, data, laws and ownership. Users should protect private information, use strong authentication, avoid plagiarism, respect copyright and behave respectfully online. Cyberbullying, piracy and unauthorized access can cause harm and may break rules or laws. In revision, focus on the key terms, understand why each concept is used, and practise explaining it with your own example.

Important Definitions and Key Terms

TermEasy definition
PrivacyA person’s ability to control access to their personal information.
CopyrightLegal protection for original creative works.
PlagiarismPresenting someone else’s work or ideas as your own.
Digital footprintThe trail of information a person leaves through online activity.

Important Short Questions with Answers

What is digital citizenship?

Responsible, safe and respectful participation in digital environments.

Differentiate copyright and plagiarism.

Copyright is a legal protection; plagiarism is presenting another’s work as one’s own.

How can users protect privacy?

Share less personal information, review permissions and use strong passwords and MFA.

What is cyberbullying?

Using digital tools to threaten, harass or humiliate someone.

Finding needs, planning a solution and using digital tools
